Hi, I'm **Bin Lei**. I'm a Ph.D. student at the **University of Minnesota (UMN)** working on large language models, with research interests spanning **LLM agents**, **code generation**, **multi-agent systems**, and **GUI visual grounding**.

My recent work includes [InfantAgent-Next](https://arxiv.org/abs/2411.01114) (a multimodal generalist agent for automated computer interaction), [GUI-Spotlight](#) (adaptive iterative focus refinement for GUI visual grounding, ICML 2025), [MACM](#) (multi-agent condition mining for complex math, NeurIPS 2024), and [AutoCoder](https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.14906) (a code LLM trained with AIEV-Instruct).

Feel free to reach me by [email](mailto:leib2765@gmail.com), or check out my code on [GitHub](https://github.com/bin123apple) and papers on [Google Scholar](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=kMu4kB4AAAAJ).
